THE BASICS

How to play

Choose 2–7 players and be the first to get rid of every card in your hand.

  1. The player holding the 3 of clubs starts the game.
  2. Play one or more cards of the same rank. For the rest of the round, each play must use at least as many cards of that same rank.
  3. You may pass, but you sit out until the trick clears. The last player to make a play leads the next trick.
  4. Threes are low. Twos are high and immediately clear the trick.
